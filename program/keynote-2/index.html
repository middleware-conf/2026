KEYNOTE 2

Carlo Curini

Azure, Spain


AI Software Factories: status, ambitions, and fears


Time & Venue

9:00 - 10:00, 17 December 2026 (Thursday)
Forum Room

Abstract

AI-assisted coding tools deliver significant acceleration of coding, but only limited impact across the end-to-end software development lifecycle (SDLC)—an Amdahl’s law effect! In this talk, I discuss our progress towards building an AI SW Factory that accelerates all the stages of SDLC—Targeting, Coding, Reviewing, and Ops. The AI SW Factory produces a metadata exhaust that enables self-improvement by fine-tuning model weights and updating a system-wide KB (a rich data substrate). We focus on Data Systems and the important class of Evolutionary Coding Tasks (i.e., those with a measurable objective to hill-climb) and report on 1) scaled deployments at Microsoft (tens of repositories) leading to 3×engineering efficiency above agentic coding and up to 22×token efficiency, and 2) several open challenges. I conclude with some pondering about how far we can push this and some of the risks I see.

Biography


Carlo Curino is a Partner Director of Research leading the Gray Systems Lab (GSL) at Microsoft. GSL is an applied research lab working on Database, Systems and AI. Before leading GSL, Carlo was a Principal Researcher part of the Cloud and Information Services Lab (CISL) in Microsoft, Senior Research at Yahoo! Research, Postdoc at MIT, and obtained his PhD/Master/Bachelor at Politecnico di Milano, a Master at UIC, and was a visiting scholar at UCLA. Carlo's research spans formal work in DB/semantic web areas, systems and algorithmic work for DBs to the cloud, BigData infrastructure and large distributed systems, infrastructure for enterprise-grade ML, HW adjacent work for Analyitics on GPUs and accelerated networks, Agentic systems and fine-tuning of LLMs for modern enterprise. Carlo is active in academic circles (100+ publications, 12K+ citations, several awards, and Program/General Chair/PC member roles for top DB/Systems conferences), and in Open Source (Apache Hadoop and Vortex PMC, REEF and Substrait committer and more).
